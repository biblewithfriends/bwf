Statement of faith

What we believe

The common faith that every believer shares, and the ground of our fellowship.

  1. The Bible is the complete, inspired Word of God.“All Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for conviction, for correction, for instruction in righteousness…”2 Tim. 3:16
  2. God is uniquely one, yet the Father, the Son and the Spirit.“…baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit…”Matt. 28:19
  3. Jesus Christ is the Son of God, who became a man, died on the cross for our sins and rose bodily from the dead.“And the Word became flesh and tabernacled among us…”John 1:14; see also 1 Cor. 15:3-4
  4. Salvation is by grace through faith in Christ, not by our own works.“For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this not of yourselves; it is the gift of God…”Eph. 2:8
  5. The church is the Body of Christ, and every believer is a member of it.“Which is His Body, the fullness of the One who fills all in all.”Eph. 1:23
  6. Christ is coming again to receive His believers to Himself.“And if I go and prepare a place for you, I am coming again and will receive you to Myself, so that where I am you also may be.”John 14:3

Verses quoted from the Holy Bible, Recovery Version.
