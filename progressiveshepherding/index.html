    Welcome to Progressive Shepherding

    Walk with each believer step by step, from being saved to seeing the key visions, the way a seed takes root and grows.

    1. Add a believer you're shepherding.
    2. Open a level and check off each item as you go through it together.
    3. Next up always shows the next step for that person.

    Want to try it first? Turn on Tour at the top to explore on sample data anytime. Nothing is saved.

    Shared with your team. Everyone who opens your group's link sees the same list and can update it, so you all know where each believer is. Keep the link within your serving team.

    Use this for your own group

    Your group's list lives in your own Google Sheet. It takes about five minutes, and there's no password: whoever has your group's link can use it.

    1. Make a new Google Sheet at sheets.new.
    2. In the sheet, open Extensions › Apps Script. Delete what's there and paste the backend code.
    3. Click Deploy › New deployment, choose Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy and allow access.
    4. Copy the Web app URL, paste it below and click Connect. You'll get your group's link to share.
